Just For You 💌

Dear Anshu,

Today, we completed 365 days of friendship. When I think about that number, it honestly feels strange. One whole year has passed since you became a part of my life, and somehow, within these 365 days, there are so many memories that it feels like we've known each other for much longer. I still remember how everything started. A simple Instagram conversation, a few messages, and then those conversations that somehow never seemed to end. Then came our calls, our video calls, our late-night talks, the random conversations about absolutely nothing, the laughter, the jokes, the little things we shared without even realizing that one day they would become memories. And if there is one thing I am genuinely grateful for, it is having known you. Thank you for being there in the moments when I needed someone to talk to. Thank you for listening to my nonsense, tolerating my overthinking, laughing at my stupid jokes, and being a part of so many ordinary days that became special because you were there. I will always remember the little things too. The food you cooked for me. The medicines you brought for me. The times you cared about whether I was okay. The conversations that lasted much longer than they were supposed to. The moments when we laughed over the smallest things. The memories we created without planning them. Maybe these things looked small at the time, but they weren't small to me. You became a very important part of one particular year of my life, and I don't think I could ever completely separate this year from the memories I have of you. There are people we meet and eventually forget. Then there are people who leave behind memories that stay with us even when life changes. You are one of those people for me. I don't know where life will take either of us from here. Maybe we'll remain exactly as we are, maybe life will become busier, maybe we'll meet less often, or maybe we'll look back at this year many years from now and smile at how different we were. But whatever happens, I hope you always know one thing: I will always be grateful that our paths crossed. Thank you for being my friend. Thank you for the memories. Thank you for the care. Thank you for all the little moments that you probably don't even remember but that I do. And most importantly, thank you for being you. 365 days are complete (as of 19 Aug), but the memories we've made during them will always remain a beautiful chapter of my life. Here's to the conversations we had, the laughter we shared, the memories we created, and all the ordinary moments that became extraordinary simply because we experienced them together. Happy 365 days of friendship, Anshu. Some people become memories. Some become chapters. And some become a part of your story. You will always be a beautiful part of mine. You were the girl in the parrot neon top and blue jeans, standing confidently during induction, not knowing that somewhere in the crowd, someone had already started a story he would remember until end of his life.

Yours,
Kartik ❤️
